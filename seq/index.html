What it is

Stock sound

The 24 engines, HARMONICS, TIMBRE, MORPH and the low-pass gate are the official Plaits 1.2 code, untouched.

A melody that writes itself

16 steps over a chord progression, always in a minor key. Hold LEFT for a new one.

TRIG = clock

16th notes from your clock. Start it and the pattern plays from step 1. No cable: 130 BPM.

V/OCT = key

0 V is C minor. Every semitone up is a new minor key, so a sequencer can change the key.

What every control does

Tap the picture to open it full size.

Every control in words
FREQUENCYThe envelope: left a short pluck, noon a medium note, right a long note; the last third adds a soft attack that always fits in the step. Hold LEFT + turn: octave 1–5 (one LED).
HARMONICS · TIMBRE · MORPHStock, for each engine. Hold LEFT + TIMBRE: low-pass gate colour. Hold RIGHT + HARMONICS: fine tune ±1 semitone (centre = in tune).
Small knob under TIMBREDENSITY: how many of the 16 steps play (fully left = silent). Off-beats come first.
Small knob under FMLENGTH: 1–16 steps.
Small knob under MORPHSPEED: 1/2 · 1/4 · 1/8 · 1/8T · 1/16 (centre) · 1/16T · 1/32 · 1/32T steps per clock.
Small knobs with a cableWhen their CV jack (TIMBRE, FM, MORPH) is patched they are the stock attenuverters again, and the sequencer keeps its last setting.
Tap LEFT / RIGHTStock: LEFT steps through the green engines, RIGHT through the red. Tap both together to switch to stepping through all 24 (LEFT back, RIGHT forward), for the yellow engines.
Hold LEFT 1 s, releaseNEW pattern (the LEDs pulse green while you hold, then sweep).
Hold both 2 sStock CV calibration.
LEDsThe engine, in its bank colour; it blinks on every note. Moving a control shows its value for a second: envelope = yellow bar, density = green bar, length = yellow bar, speed = one LED (red for triplets).
TRIGClock in, 16ths. When the clock stops for 4 clock periods (0.5–4.5 s) the pattern goes back to step 1 and waits; plugging the cable in does the same. No cable: 130 BPM.
V/OCTThe key: 0 V (or nothing) = C minor, +1 semitone = C♯ minor, +7 = G minor… Taken on every step.
LEVELA VCA after the envelope: the sequence keeps playing.
MODEL · HARMO · OUT · AUXStock. MODEL CV is taken on each note.
03

How it plays

  1. Harmony. Each pattern has 1, 2 or 4 sections on minor-key chords (mostly i, iv, v, VI, III).
  2. Melody. A random walk over two octaves of the scale on top of them, landing on chord tones often.
  3. Ranks. Every step gets a rank; DENSITY plays the steps ranked below it, so turning it up adds notes without changing the ones you hear.
  4. Accents. Some steps are accented (a little louder and brighter, like the stock LEVEL accent).
  5. Saved. Engine, octave, fine tune, colour and the pattern come back at power-on.

First release: the official Plaits 1.2 engines with a generative melody sequencer. Tested in a Cortex-M4 emulator: boot, panel, clock, save / restore, every engine (no added clipping: the modal and snare engines clip on loud accents exactly as stock Plaits does), the same CPU load as stock Plaits, 5 KB of stack to spare.
