KRYPTA

A stereo drone voice for the deep end. Nine algorithms, chords that never leave the key, a frequency shifter, a metallic DUNGEON and a diffuse REFLEKTA space. Sequence it (STEP, TURING, METROPOLIS, DUB) or let it just drone (DRONE).

Headphones or a real system recommended — a lot of it lives under 100 Hz.
