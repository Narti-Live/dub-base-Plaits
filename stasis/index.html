Slow swells, drones, wind, metal and dust that never repeat and never follow a tempo. G minor, or nowhere at all. One page: eight macros, a guide, your hands.

Constant loudness, never above −3 dBFS. Start with your mixer channel down, then bring it up.
Original code, inspired by the idea of cloud-oscillator modules (Synthesis Technology E352) and the dark ambient tradition; not affiliated with Synthesis Technology, Cryo Chamber or Elektron. Narti Live, MIT license.
