Monsoon visual manual

What every knob, fader, button and jack does in the three new modes, with a sound example for each control. Tap a control on the panel or in the list. The VHIKK examples are the module on its own with no input; the CAVERN and PHASER examples process the Dub Stab Cm DEEP chords.

Each label shows what that control does in this mode; the number matches the list. Red = Vhikk X-inspired functions. A label with a bar (WARP|SPAN) is the knob alone | with the LED button held. Dark ring = selected.

    All eight modes

    Open mode select: press the LED button and LOAD/SAVE together, or hold the LED button for 2.5 s. Then LOAD/SAVE steps to the next mode and the LED button to the previous one. To leave, hold the LED button about 1 s, then about 1 s again. The four LEDs show which mode you are on.

    Install and restore

    Flash v1.3

    Hold FREEZE while you power on. The LEDs show the bootloader. Play Monsoon-NartiLive-v1.3.wav into IN L from a phone or computer at full volume, with no other sounds playing. The module restarts when it is done.

    Go back to stock

    Flash Monsoon-ORIGINAL-Parasites-v2.01-restore.wav the same way. Your saved buffers are kept.

    Buttons, everywhere

    LED button: tap = meter display, hold 1 s = quality menu, hold 2.5 s = mode select. In VHIKK a tap changes the algorithm and holding it is the shift for SPAN, FUSE and SCAN. FREEZE: tap = freeze, hold 1 s = reverse (granular modes).
